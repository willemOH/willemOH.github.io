WILLEM HILLIARD
  • I'm Willem Hilliard — a technologist, artist, and educator based in the Pacific Northwest. My work lives at the intersection of hardware, software, and human connection.

    I studied Computer Science and Visual Arts at Bennington College, and since then my path has wandered through some unexpected places: mentoring teens in film production in rural Maine, supporting students at the Texas School for the Blind and Visually Impaired, designing virtual reality experiences in London, and taking a Sundance-bound project across the finish line.

    These days I run my own technology venture out of Bellingham, WA, where I invent and engineer musical electronic instruments — most notably SoundPouch, an original device I've developed end to end, from software to PCB design. I also design open-source music player hardware (Phoniebox) with an eye toward bringing it to market. When I'm not soldering or pushing code, I take on landscaping and handyman work to keep the runway long.
